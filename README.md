# 404

because apparently your 404 page is the only thing people will actually look at when things go wrong.

## what is this?

a collection of actually good design ideas for 404 pages. you know, those error pages that somehow get more love than your homepage. this repo has templates, design patterns, and code you can steal (i mean *borrow*) to make your 404 pages not look like they were designed in 1999 by internet explorer.

## what you get

- **clean design templates** - the kind that don't make users want to close the tab immediately
- **responsive code** - works on phones, tablets, desktops, and probably your smart fridge too
- **copy that doesn't suck** - because "page not found" is boring
- **animation ideas** - the fun kind that aren't excessive and actually serve a purpose (radical, i know)
- **accessibility** - so everyone gets to experience your 404 page equally (fun times for all)

## how to use this

1. pick a design you vibe with
2. copy the code (go ahead, no one's watching)
3. customize it with your own colors, copy, whatever
4. star it for more updates
5. ship it and watch your error pages become the highlight of your website
